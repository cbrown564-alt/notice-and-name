## Positioning

Private vocabulary instrument: language, reflection, partner-safe phrases, on-device.

Under-served vs:

- demo libraries (OMGyes-class)
- therapy / audio stacks (subscription apps)

Not entertainment; not a therapy session. A personal naming tool that stays private.

## Pricing decision

- **Free download** with useful private preview
- **One non-consumable** full unlock at **£15**
- **No subscription** at launch

£15 sits under OMGyes pay-once pricing, still premium vs tip-jar.
