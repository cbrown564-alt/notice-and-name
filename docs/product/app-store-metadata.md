## Full description

Pleasure Vocabulary helps you recognize an experience, learn a useful word for it, decide
whether it fits, and keep language you may want to use later.

**What you get**

- 22 curated concepts across techniques, sensations, timing, psychology, and anatomy
- Five guided pathways and four research explainers
- Personal statuses, private field notes, and saved partner-safe phrases
- Warm illustrations, video, and interactive native diagrams where they help explain an idea

**Designed for privacy**

- No account required
- No analytics on what you read or mark as resonant
- Optional biometric app lock
- Export and delete-all-data controls

**Tone**

Warm, editorial, and evidence-based—not clinical, not sensational. Built for people who want language and understanding, not explicit demos.

## Review notes (App Store Connect)

This app is an **educational vocabulary tool** about adult sexual wellness. It does not
contain pornography, explicit demonstrations, a social feed, or shared user-generated
content. Illustrations are abstract or educational. The age-rating questionnaire includes
frequent mature themes; the final rating is generated by App Store Connect.

The app offers a non-consumable full-library unlock. The free preview and purchase entry
point are visible in Explore; no account or test login is required. Provide an App Review
promo code or review configuration for the unlock when the product is created.

Test account: not required (no server login).
