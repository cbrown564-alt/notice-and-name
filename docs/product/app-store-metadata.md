## Full description

Pleasure Vocabulary Builder helps you name what you feel. Each concept is a five-step journey: recognize the experience, learn the word, see it illustrated, understand the research, and reflect on what resonates.

**What you get**

- 22 curated concepts across techniques, sensations, timing, psychology, and anatomy
- Guided pathways for self-discovery and partner communication
- A private journal and profile that stays on your device
- Export a shareable summary of concepts that resonate with you

**Designed for privacy**

- No account required
- No analytics on what you read or mark as resonant
- Optional biometric app lock (settings)

**Tone**

Warm, editorial, and evidence-based—not clinical, not sensational. Built for people who want language and understanding, not explicit demos.
