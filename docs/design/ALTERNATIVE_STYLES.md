# Alternative Visual Style Explorations

During the design review of Phase 1.3 (Image Pilots) in May 2026, alternative styling directions were explored to address potential concerns about clinical literalism. The final decision was to stick with the primary **Scientific Warmth** textbook style with improved prompt execution.

**Jul 2026:** Exploratory PNGs were removed from the repo after confirming production plates remain canonical. This doc is retained as a record of directions considered, not assets on disk.

---
