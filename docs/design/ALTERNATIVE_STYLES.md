# Alternative Visual Style Explorations

During the design review of Phase 1.3 (Image Pilots) in May 2026, alternative styling directions were explored to address potential concerns about clinical literalism. Although the final decision was to stick with the primary **Scientific Warmth** textbook style with improved prompt execution, these explorations have been archived in the repository for reference.

## 1. Botanical / Coral Metaphor
Replaces literal internal anatomy and body outlines with botanical analogies (translucent flowers, corals, branching riverways).

*   **Clitoral Structure (Orchid):** [botanical-clitoral.png](file:///Users/cobro/code/Pleasure%20Vocabulary%20Builder/assets/images/concepts/illustrations/alternative_styles/botanical-clitoral.png)
    *   *Concept:* Wing-like orchid petals curve around an empty central space (canal), with coral-glowing veins tracing nerve paths.
*   **Spreading Sensation (Sea-Fan Coral):** [botanical-spreading.png](file:///Users/cobro/code/Pleasure%20Vocabulary%20Builder/assets/images/concepts/illustrations/alternative_styles/botanical-spreading.png)
    *   *Concept:* A glowing point of golden light at the base propagates outward along the coral capillaries.

## 2. Hand-Drawn Sketchbook / Journal
Replaces digital vector rendering with loose graphite sketches, charcoal shading, and watercolor fills on high-texture paper.

*   **Clitoral Structure (Pencil/Watercolor):** [sketchbook-clitoral.png](file:///Users/cobro/code/Pleasure%20Vocabulary%20Builder/assets/images/concepts/illustrations/alternative_styles/sketchbook-clitoral.png)
    *   *Concept:* Loose graphite line-work shaded with charcoal, with a coral/rose watercolor bloom representing blood flow.
*   **Spreading Sensation (Silhouette/Wash):** [sketchbook-spreading.png](file:///Users/cobro/code/Pleasure%20Vocabulary%20Builder/assets/images/concepts/illustrations/alternative_styles/sketchbook-spreading.png)
    *   *Concept:* Pencil outline of a torso with golden-amber watercolor pigments radiating across the hips.
*   **Desk/Notebook Composition (Literal):** [sketchbook-spreading-desk.png](file:///Users/cobro/code/Pleasure%20Vocabulary%20Builder/assets/images/concepts/illustrations/alternative_styles/sketchbook-spreading-desk.png)
    *   *Concept:* A literal representation showing a notebook open on a wooden table next to a pencil.
