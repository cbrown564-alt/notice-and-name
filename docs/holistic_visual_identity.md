# Holistic Visual Identity: The "Living Journal"

## 1. The Core DNA
The overarching aesthetic of the Pleasure Vocabulary Builder is **"Scientific Warmth."** 

It bridges two worlds that are often separate: 
1.  **The Medical/Academic**: Authoritative, precise, and grounded.
2.  **The Spiritual/Somatic**: Organic, fluid, glowing, and felt.

The app should feel like a **high-end wellness journal** discovered in a modern laboratory. It is not clinical (cold/sterile), nor is it purely decorative (vacuous/trendy). Every visual element serves to make the invisible sensation *visible* and *safe*.

### shared_constants
Across all distinct visual layers, these rules are immutable:
*   **The Canvas**: No stark white bounds. Backgrounds are always "Paper"—creamy off-whites, textured cardstocks, or warm beiges (`neutral[50]`).
*   **The Tone**: Gentle, non-judgmental, and safe. No aggressive reds or warning signs.
*   **The Metaphor**: "Bioluminescence." Life and sensation are represented by light/glow, not by anatomical gore.

## 2. Distinct Visual Layers

While the DNA is shared, the visual expression shifts depending on the *function* of the screen.

### Layer A: The Interface (The Container)
*Defined in `app_visual_identity.md`*

**Role**: The polite host. It frames the content without competing with it.
*   **Vibe**: "Modern Wellness Editorial." Think *Kinfolk* meets *Headspace*.
*   **Typography**:
    *   **Headings**: High-contrast Serif (e.g., Playfair Display). Literary and magazine-like.
    *   **Body**: Clean, legible Sans-Serif.
    *   **Voice**: Italicized serif for "whispered" questions or intimate prompts.
*   **Palette**: Earth Tones. Terracotta, Sage, Sand.
*   **Shape Language**: Soft, rounded cards. Significant whitespace to allow "breathing room."

### Layer B: Concept Illustrations (The Theory)
*Defined in `asset_visual_identities.md` ("The Renaissance")*

**Role**: The "Textbook" Plate. Used deeply within a concept to explain *what* it is.
*   **Vibe**: "Renaissance-Meets-Bioluminescence."
*   **Visual Signature**:
    *   **Line Work**: Fine, pencil-like striations and cross-hatching (Etching style). Representing structure/bone/anatomy.
    *   **Lighting**: Ethereal, glowing "magic" overlays. Representing nerve/sensation/blood.
*   **Color Use**:
    *   **Warmth (Peach/Gold)** = Embodied connection.
    *   **Cool (Metallic Blue)** = Detached observation (Mind).

### Layer C: Library Thumbnails (The Glimpse)
*Defined in `asset_visual_identities.md` ("The Feeling")*

**Role**: Abstract identifiers. Quick visual cues in the list view.
*   **Vibe**: "Bio-Fluidity" & "Tactile Digital."
*   **Visual Signature**:
    *   **Abstraction**: No literal anatomy. Shapes are organic blobs, ripples, and ribbons.
    *   **Texture**: Matte grain/noise. Looks like airbrushed paper, not glossy 3D plastic.
*   **Movement**: Even if static, the shapes imply flow (liquid dynamics).

### Layer D: Interactive Diagrams (The Lab)
*Defined in `animation_journey.md` & `visual_content_strategy.md`*

**Role**: The Tool. Simplified for physics and clarity.
*   **Vibe**: "Interactive Physics."
*   **Visual Signature**:
    *   **Simplicity**: Stripped back to essential forms to allow for 60fps manipulation.
    *   **The "Antigravity Standard"**:
        *   **Anatomy/Passive**: `neutral[300]` lines.
        *   **User/Active**: `primary[500]` (Hot Coral) glow.
    *   **Feedback**: Changes in state are instantaneous and often color-coded (Grey -> Hot Coral on success).

## 3. Synthesis: How They Knit Together

The user journey moves through these layers, creating a coherent depth-of-field effect:

1.  **The Home/Library (Layer A + C)**: You are in a calm, editorial space. You see abstract, fluid shapes **(Thumbnails)** that invite you in without overwhelming you with medical detail.
2.  **The Concept Detail (Layer A + B)**: You tap in. The typography remains editorial **(UI)**, but the imagery becomes detailed and authoritative **(Illustrations/Etchings)**. You are now "studying."
3.  **The Experiment (Layer D)**: You engage a "Simulator." The visuals strip back to pure function **(Interactive)** to let you focus on the mechanics of the feeling.

### Alignment Check
*   **Where they align**: All layers prioritize "Warmth" and "Light" over clinical realism.
*   **Where they diverge (intentionally)**:
    *   **Thumbnails** are *abstract* to be approachable.
    *   **Illustrations** are *detailed* to be educational.
    *   **Interactives** are *simplified* to be functional.

This tiered system ensures the app encourages *feeling* (Thumbnails) -> *understanding* (Illustrations) -> *mastering* (Interactives).
