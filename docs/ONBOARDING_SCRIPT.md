## 1. Welcome

This is Notice and Name.

A private place to notice what you like, and find words for it.

Everything stays on your phone. No account. No feed. No one watching.

## 2. The problem

Wanting can be hard to name.

When the words are vague, it is hard to ask for what feels good — even with yourself.

## 3. The loop

Here is the simple loop.

Notice what you feel.

Name it with a clear word.

Reflect a little.

Keep the phrases that actually fit you.

## 4. How it feels

Short concepts, not a course.

Each one helps you recognize a pattern in your body or desire,

then leaves you with language you might say out loud later — if you want.

## 5. Privacy

You can lock the app.

You can export your notes.

You can delete everything.

Your reflections are yours alone.

## 6. Into the app

Start with a free concept. See if the voice fits.

When you are ready, unlock the full library once — no subscription.
